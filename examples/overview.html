prose / notes

This interface is meant to run for days on hardware you own: a Pi in the studio, a NUC in the basement, a tiny VM on the intranet. The type stays at one base size most of the time — weight and color carry hierarchy, not headline inflation.

Longer copy still belongs in the same monospace voice. Think release notes, irrigation schedules, or a short explanation of why a service is down — not marketing paragraphs.
